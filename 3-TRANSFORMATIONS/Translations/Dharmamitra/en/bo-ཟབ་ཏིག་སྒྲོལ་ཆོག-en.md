# bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག — DharmaMitra zero-shot (english) ^0

## ༄༅། །བཅོམ་ལྡན་འདས་ཤཱཀྱའི་རྒྱལ་པོ་ལ་གསོལ་བ་འདེབས་པའི་ཚིགས་སུ་བཅད་པ་བཅོ་ལྔ་པ། ^1-0

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^1-1]]

I prostrate with devotion to the Guru and Mañjughoṣa. ^1-1

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^1-2]]

Beneath the sovereign Bodhi tree, you pervaded the earth and sky, subduing the hosts of Māra,
Who rained down a shower of vajras, short spears, wheels, fire, mountains, and ranges of snow-peaks.
When the powerful one, adorned with flower-arrows, strove to obstruct your enlightenment through miraculous displays,
You defeated them well with a heart of loving-kindness; I bow my head to you, the one who attained perfect Buddhahood. ^1-2

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^1-3]]

For the sake of others,
With perfect intention and application,
When you generated the mind for supreme enlightenment,
The earth trembled in six ways. ^1-3

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^1-4]]

You have perfectly accomplished generosity and ethical discipline,
And practiced patience and diligence;
Having reached the culmination of meditative concentration and wisdom,
I prostrate to you, who have perfected the perfections. ^1-4

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^1-5]]

When the protector of beings was first born,
All the wise ones of the world rejoiced;
The hosts of Māra were filled with great anxiety,
And all the non-Buddhist extremists were terrified. ^1-5

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^1-6]]

Therefore, your teaching
Possesses enlightened activity like the light of the sun;
To you, the unerring kinsman in every respect,
I go for refuge. ^1-6

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^1-7]]

Your scriptural speech is a lion’s roar;
When proclaimed to the world including the gods,
Brahmā and Viṣṇu were rendered speechless,
And the teachers of the gods shrank away like foxes. ^1-7

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^1-8]]

The sages Akṣapāda, Vālmīki,
Vyāsa, Ne-jok, and others,
All those who had attained renown—
I bow to you, who defeated them all with the Dharma. ^1-8

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^1-9]]

Having attained supreme enlightenment,
Your work for the sake of beings is truly wondrous;
That you sacrificed your own life
Before being freed from the craving of existence makes my hair stand on end. ^1-9

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^1-10]]

Without regarding your own life,
Saying, "I shall act for your benefit,"
You cared for us who were without a protector;
O Protector, I take refuge in you. ^1-10

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^1-11]]

The Victorious One subdued the hosts of Māra,
Defeated all the non-Buddhist extremists,
Liberated countless disciples of the Hearers,
And gave prophecies to the noble heirs of the Victor. ^1-11

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^1-12]]

Seeing such excellent deeds,
And aspiring for your qualities,
I enter upon the path for the sake of enlightenment;
Once again, I take refuge in you. ^1-12

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^1-13]]

Your son is Mañjughoṣa,
Your disciple is Śāradvatīputra,
You have made Maitreya your supreme regent;
Who could possibly doubt this? ^1-13

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^1-14]]

What need is there to speak of your qualities?
Seeing the qualities of your sons, one is overcome;
By the brilliance of the rising dawn,
One understands the rays of the friend of the lotus. ^1-14

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^1-15]]

Because you have attained in every way
The phenomena as they are and as they exist,
I prostrate to you, who have accomplished
The enlightened activities as you intended. ^1-15

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^1-16]]

Thus, the Guru of beings, of the Śākya lineage,
Born from the royal line of the Ikṣvāku,
By this praise of the Bhagavan, the Perfect Buddha,
May beings swiftly attain omniscience. ^1-16

Dharmakāya Prajñāpāramitā, Bhagavatī Samantabhadrī,
Complete Sambhogakāya Vajravārāhī,
Emanation-display, the Venerable Mother Tārā,
I pray to you: grant the supreme and ordinary accomplishments! ^2-1

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^2-2]]

The second Buddha, Padmākara,
Lord of a hundred thousand ḍākinīs, holder of the secret mantra treasury,
Bodhisattva Prince Rolpa Tsal,
I pray to you: grant the supreme and ordinary accomplishments! ^2-2

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^2-3]]

Chokgyur Dechen Lingpa, who opened the door to profound treasures,
Dorje Ziji Tsal, endowed with the seven authoritative transmissions,
Pema Garwang, who clarified the Buddha’s teachings,
I pray to you: grant the supreme and ordinary accomplishments! ^2-3

Moreover, to the holders of the treasury of profound and vast ripening and liberation,
The root and lineage gurus, the Noble Goddess, the Swift One,
And the ocean of the Three Roots and Vajra Dharma protectors,
I pray to you: grant the supreme and ordinary accomplishments! ^2-5

May the field of my mind be moistened by the spirit of renunciation,
May the sprouts of pure, altruistic intention grow in lush rows,
And bearing the abundant fruit of great primordial wisdom,
I pray that you grant the spontaneous accomplishment of the twofold benefit! ^2-6

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^4-65]]

Homage to her whose fingers, in the mudrā symbolizing the Three Jewels,
Beautifully adorn her heart,
Adorned with wheels in every direction without exception,
Radiating a turbulent mass of her own brilliance. ^4-65

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^4-66]]

Homage to you, majestic in your supreme joy,
Who spread garlands of light as your crown ornament,
Who, with the laughter of TUTTĀRE,
Bring demons and the world under your power. ^4-66

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^4-67]]

Homage to you, who have the power
To summon all the hosts of local guardians,
Who, with a vibrating frown and the syllable HŪṀ,
Completely liberate from all destitution. ^4-67

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^4-68]]

Homage to you, whose head is adorned with a crescent moon,
Whose every ornament blazes intensely,
From whose matted locks Amitābha
Constantly radiates a magnificent light. ^4-68

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^4-69]]

Homage to you, who dwell in the midst of a blazing garland,
Like the fire at the end of an eon;
With right leg extended and left bent, surrounded by joy,
You completely destroy the armies of enemies. ^4-69

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^4-70]]

Homage to you, who strike the surface of the earth
With your palms and trample it with your feet;
With a wrathful frown and the syllable HŪṀ,
You shatter the seven levels. ^4-70

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^4-71]]

Homage to you, blissful, virtuous, and peaceful,
Whose very sphere of experience is the peace of nirvāṇa;
Perfectly endowed with SVĀHĀ and OṀ,
You are the one who destroys great negativity. ^4-71

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^4-72]]

Homage to you, surrounded by intense joy,
Who utterly shatters the bodies of enemies;
With the ten-syllable mantra arranged on your speech,
You are the wisdom-Tara arising from HŪṀ. ^4-72

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^4-73]]

Homage to you, who stamp your feet with the syllable TURE,
Whose essence is the seed-syllable in the form of HŪṀ;
You cause Mount Meru, Mandara, and Vindhya,
And all the three worlds to tremble. ^4-73

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^4-74]]

Homage to you, who hold in your hand the deer-marked moon,
Which has the form of a celestial lake;
By reciting TĀRA twice and the syllable PHAT,
You clear away every poison without exception. ^4-74

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^4-75]]

Homage to you, the sovereign of the hosts of gods,
Served by gods and kinnaras;
With the majesty of your armor of universal joy,
You dispel strife and bad dreams. ^4-75

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^4-76]]

Homage to you, whose two eyes are as brilliant
As the light of the full sun and moon;
By reciting HARA twice and TUTTĀRE,
You dispel the most violent contagious diseases. ^4-76

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^4-77]]

Homage to you, perfectly endowed with the power of peace
Through the arrangement of the three suchnesses;
You are the supreme TURE who destroys
The hosts of demons, zombies, and yakṣas. ^4-77

## ༄༅། །འཕགས་མ་ཡིད་བཞིན་འཁོར་ལོའི་ཐུགས་དམ་གནད་ནས་བསྐུལ་བའི་གསོལ་འདེབས་མོས་གུས་ཀྱི་ཏམྦུ་ར་ཞེས་བྱ་བ། ^5-0

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-1]]

From the ocean of Avalokiteśvara’s compassion,
The glory of the blooming lotus of benefit and bliss has arisen;
Noble Tārā, possessing the light of nectar,
Please pacify these waves of darkness filled with fear. ^5-1

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-2]]

Mother of all the Victors, medicine that dispels torment,
Loving nurse, the sole kinsman of all living beings;
Throughout the garland of my lives, I have no other,
So I take refuge in you until I reach the heart of enlightenment. ^5-2

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-3]]

For us and all beings tormented by our karma,
Wherever we dwell, when sickness, weapons, famine, and the like
Repeatedly afflict us with countless unwanted sufferings,
Please pull us from this mire of misery, O Jetsunma. ^5-3

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-4]]

Conceptual thoughts following habitual tendencies are like ripples on water;
In each and every instant, I accumulate many causes for the miserable realms.
Of this never-ending seed of cyclic existence,
Please cut the root right now, O Jetsunma. ^5-4

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-5]]

Because the great accumulation of merit has not preceded this,
Though I boast of having entered the gateway of the Victor’s teachings,
My trust in the Three Jewels has not been firmly established;
Please uproot these karmic obscurations, O Jetsunma. ^5-5

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-6]]

Even when I see that the fruits of karma are ripening before my eyes,
I still have not developed faith in karma and its results;
Like a beast of prey in saffron robes,
Please lead my mind toward virtue, O Jetsunma. ^5-6

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-7]]

The human body, impermanent by day and night, moment by moment,
Has been attained, yet the great purpose remains unaccomplished;
The continuous noose of the Lord of Death, so difficult to turn back,
Please sever with the sword of wisdom, O Jetsunma. ^5-7

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-8]]

Even in the short term, from demons, epidemics, poisons, and weapons,
From sudden death and base conditions,
And from the eight and sixteen fears,
Please protect me quickly, O Jetsunma. ^5-8

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-9]]

As long as the stream of mind is habituated to the evil of self-grasping,
For that long, the river of suffering will constantly flow;
Therefore, regarding the nature of the aggregates and so forth,
Please ignite the lamp of realization, O Jetsunma. ^5-9

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-10]]

May I recognize all beings as my mothers, with love and compassion,
Not just in words, but by truly exchanging the suffering of others
With my own happiness; O Jetsunma, please cultivate in me
The vast and great courage of those who do this. ^5-10

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-11]]

In all my lifetimes, may I meet a spiritual friend who is not mistaken,
And through them understand all the teachings;
From now until enlightenment, O Jetsunma, please destroy
The demonic forces that obstruct my life and liberation. ^5-11

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-12]]

Possessing the eye of wisdom, free from the dust of profound insight,
And the excellent conduct of one whose afflictions gradually diminish,
With great respect for the training, and with mindfulness and vigilance,
O Jetsunma, please blend my mind-stream with the Dharma. ^5-12

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-13]]

When the momentum of my life finally reaches its end,
Without fear, having realized the ten perceptions,
Into the basic space free from the extremes of birth and cessation,
O Jetsunma, please introduce me to the nature of clear light. ^5-13

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-14]]

Even if the bardo appears for but a fleeting moment,
May the emanation and reabsorption of the pure mandala be complete;
In the Lotus Array of Great Bliss and other such realms,
O Jetsunma, please lead me to a pure land. ^5-14

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-15]]

Except for taking birth in existence through my own intention,
May I not wander in cyclic existence by the power of karma;
In the presence of the Victorious One of Infinite Life and Light,
O Jetsunma, please prophesy my path and guide me forward. ^5-15

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-16]]

Oceans of Buddhas, both those who have come and those yet to come,
Hold the holy Dharma as their life-force in all infinite realms;
O Jetsunma, please grant that my aspirations be fulfilled,
Which teach the supreme conduct of awakening to perfection. ^5-16

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-17]]

All the misdeeds, obscurations, faults, and downfalls accumulated since beginningless time,
May they be purified and cleansed within this physical support;
And until that time, grant me the glory of harmony with the Dharma,
And the siddhi of deathless life-empowerment, O Jetsunma. ^5-17

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-18]]

Even in dreams, may the supreme mind of awakening
Not decline, and having completely abandoned all the works of Māra,
May whatever I do with my three doors be a source
That spreads benefit and happiness for the teachings and beings, O Jetsunma. ^5-18

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-19]]

Having completely abandoned the distractions of the eight worldly concerns of this life,
And without obstacles in the forests of the remote wilderness,
By condensing the practice into its essence, in this very life,
Make this human existence meaningful, O Jetsunma. ^5-19

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-20]]

Through this suffering that I am currently experiencing,
May the causes and effects of the suffering of all beings
Be swiftly exhausted, and from now on, may I lead all beings, myself and others,
From happiness to ever-greater happiness, O Jetsunma. ^5-20

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-21]]

May the supreme teachings of the Victor, the source of benefit and happiness,
Flourish and spread throughout the ten directions without ever declining,
And may the lives of all those sublime great beings who uphold them
Be extended for a long time, O Jetsunma. ^5-21

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-22]]

In particular, the profound meaning of the essential definitive truth,
May those with the good fortune to hear it have it settle in their ears;
May the hosts of gods, nāgas, and yakṣas who delight in this,
Raise its status to the very heights of heaven, O Jetsunma. ^5-22

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-23]]

May the very name of unhappiness be absent throughout all lands,
And may lifespan, merit, and glory flourish, with reverence for the teachings;
From the great clouds of the jewel of a golden age of happiness,
Let fall a rain of all that is desired, O Jetsunma. ^5-23

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-24]]

For the sake of the Victor’s teachings and the happiness of sentient beings,
May all the dense thickets of humans and non-humans
Who engage in harmful thoughts and actions
Be burned by the flames of your power and might, O Jetsunma. ^5-24

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-25]]

May all mothers, vast as space, be free from the fears
Of untimely death and the like, and naturally enjoy the glory of the ten virtues;
May they accompany the foundation of the Three Jewels’ teachings,
And steer the helm of liberation upward, O Jetsunma. ^5-25

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-26]]

In particular, may I and those who depend upon me
Never fall under the power of obstacles at any time;
And may the pillar of life, firm and enduring,
That perfects longevity and practice, be established, O Jetsunma. ^5-26

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-27]]

O Deity in whom I have placed my trust for this life and the next,
O Bhagavatī, who has become the Mother of the Victors of the three times,
If you do not bring the fruits of my prayers to fruition,
I have no other refuge, O Jetsunma. ^5-27

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-28]]

By the power of the two accumulations gathered since beginningless time,
You have attained an inconceivable body of space;
Because I pray with one-pointed longing,
Do not diminish your compassionate blessings, O Jetsunma. ^5-28

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-29]]

Your body is the full autumn moon in the center of a lotus,
The youthful bloom of vajra-bliss perfectly complete;
With an infinite mandala of stainless light,
Bestow the enlightened activities of the four rites, O Jetsunma. ^5-29

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-30]]

With your palm that grants the supreme gift of fulfilling all wishes—
Through which touching brings Dharma, wealth, desire, and liberation,
And which is formed from a hundred merits—
Anoint the crown of my head, O Jetsunma. ^5-30

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-31]]

Upon the tip of the precious stem between the thumb and ring finger,
From a lovely lotus that has newly blossomed,
You pour forth nectar of every supreme and common kind;
Bring delight to my mind, O Jetsunma. ^5-31

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-32]]

Adorned with nets of pearls and small golden bells,
Displaying the graceful play of divine ornaments and the like,
Blazing with the majesty of molten gold at dawn,
Appear before the eyes of my faith, O Jetsunma. ^5-32

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-33]]

Supreme among those who grant rest in infinite realms,
With a backdrop of the stainless, full moon,
Seated in the indestructible vajra posture,
Dwell as the ornament upon the crown of my head, O Jetsunma. ^5-33

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-34]]

Proclaiming the Dharma with the profound melody of Brahmā,
And teaching the vast, uninterrupted continuity,
Sever all the webs of the three obscurations,
And grant the relief of the throne of enlightenment, O Jetsunma. ^5-34

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^5-35]]

In short, since you are the essence of refuge,
Until I attain enlightenment, always
Do not let me fall under the power of obstructing demons,
But grant your grace to perfect the twofold benefit. ^5-35

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^7-2]]

With the stainless, precious vessel of your compassion,

You are the supreme essence churned from the ocean of the two accumulations;

I prostrate to the Noble Lady, the Wish-Fulfilling Wheel. ^7-3

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^7-4]]

From the singular lapis lazuli lute of your secret speech,
The ocean of melodic branches, like music,
Arises fully as the radiance of ten million Dharma-heaps;
I prostrate to the Noble Lady, the Wish-Fulfilling Wheel. ^7-4

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^7-5]]

Your heart-mind is the expanse of non-dual primordial wisdom;
Though you have withdrawn the engagement and emergence of conceptual elaborations,
You possess boundless splendor of awareness and love;
I prostrate to the Noble Lady, the Wish-Fulfilling Wheel. ^7-5

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^7-6]]

Possessing the grace of the sixty-four inexhaustible qualities,

You are the Mother who captivates all the Victors and their heirs;
I prostrate to the Noble Lady, the Wish-Fulfilling Wheel. ^7-6

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^7-7]]

Constant in continuity and all-pervading like space,
Through spontaneously accomplished enlightened activity,
You fulfill the hopes of those to be trained, O Lord of the Wish-Fulfilling Tree;
I prostrate to the Noble Lady, the Wish-Fulfilling Wheel. ^7-7

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^7-8]]

In short, for the sake of fully perfecting
The compassionate resolve and sacred vows of all who possess the ten powers,
You perform inconceivable deeds to tame beings,
I prostrate to the Noble Lady, the Wish-Fulfilling Wheel. ^7-8

From the great clouds of your wisdom-vision, perfect and complete,
Let the rain of all desired accomplishments fall,
And grant your blessings so that the festival of benefit and happiness may flourish. ^7-9

## ༄༅། །ཀུན་མཁྱེན་ཐུབ་པའི་བསྟན་པ་རྒྱས་པའི་སྨོན་ལམ་དྲང་སྲོང་ལྷ་ཡི་བདེན་ཚིག་ཅེས་བྱ་བ་བཞུགས་སོ། ། ^9-0

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^9-1]]

Homage to all gurus, buddhas, and bodhisattvas. ^9-1

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^9-2]]

More distinguished than the thousand buddhas of this fortunate aeon,
Noble and wondrous as the uḍumbara flower,
You who took hold of this realm during this age of strife,
Homage to the Supreme Sage, the Fulfiller of All Aims. ^9-2

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^9-3]]

Buddhas, bodhisattvas, śrāvakas, and pratyekabuddhas of the ten directions,
Mañjuśrī, Avalokiteśvara, and the Holder of the Vajra,
Bodhisattvas such as Maitreya and the rest,
The sixteen elders and the seven great successors. ^9-3

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^9-4]]

The supreme ornaments of Jambudvīpa and the wondrous great masters,
The eighty-four lords of yoga and the rest,

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^9-5]]

The five Sakya patriarchs, Marpa, Milarepa, and Gampopa,
Mañjughoṣa Lama and all the other Indian and Tibetan
Assemblies of learned and accomplished vidyādharas,
The peaceful and wrathful yidams, ḍākinīs, dharma protectors, and so forth—
I invoke you from the depths of your heart; look upon us with your compassion! ^9-5

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^9-6]]

The Buddha's teachings of the Vinaya, Sūtra, and Abhidharma,
Along with the four great classes of the Secret Mantra Tantras,
The ten great pillars of the scriptural lineages in the Land of Snows,
And the eight great chariots of the practice lineages, and so forth—
Behold how all the systems of the Buddha’s teachings, both Sūtra and Mantra,
Have now fallen into a state of extreme decline! ^9-6

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^9-7]]

Those who bear the victory banner of saffron robes and the discipline of renunciation,
Bodhisattvas who possess genuine, unfeigned altruism,
And mantra-holders of the generation and completion stages who maintain their samaya—
To those who have become like a painted lamp,
You who possess the eye of primordial wisdom,
Please look upon us with certainty and protect us with your compassion! ^9-7

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^9-8]]

Sentient beings of this degenerate age, when the five drosses are rampant,
Are bound by the powerful force of afflictions and the shackles of craving.
Those who have entered the teachings are driven by the red wind of jealousy;
The teachings are agitated from within, and the dregs of degenerate views flourish. ^9-8

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^9-9]]

Because it has become an age of contention, the pollution of those who undermine the teachings
Causes the Dharma protectors and guardians of the virtuous side to become indifferent.
Throughout every land, asuras and barbarians abound.
Through these and similar circumstances, the teachings are being destroyed. ^9-9

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^9-10]]

Alas! O compassionate Protector,
Exert the power and might of your sacred vows,
Why would you ever be discouraged by the sorrows of these degenerate times? ^9-10

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^9-11]]

Since you are lords of the ten strengths and possess the ten masteries,
Restore these declining teachings to their original state,
And may those who are authoritative, learned, disciplined, and accomplished,
Manifest in many forms to restore the teachings. ^9-11

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^9-12]]

In both India and Tibet, may the precious lamp
Of the festival of a later golden age be lit,
And may the lotus grove of the Buddha's non-sectarian teachings reach its full bloom. ^9-12

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^9-13]]

May the minds of the hostile throngs be transformed,
And may all aspire to the teachings and engage in the ten virtues.
May the Sangha who uphold the teachings pervade the entire world,
And may study and practice flourish and spread. ^9-13

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^9-14]]

May the temples be filled with vast clouds of offerings,
And the great drum of the sacred Dharma resound loudly.
Shattering the skulls of the heretical elephants,
May the Buddha’s teachings be victorious in all directions! ^9-14

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^9-15]]

May the lives of the masters who uphold the teachings be firm,
And may the lifespan and merit of the patrons of the teachings increase.
May those who harm the teachings be reduced to nothing but their names,
And may the victory banner of the teachings be raised in the ten directions! ^9-15

## ༄༅། །ཇ་མཆོད། ^10-0

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^10-1]]

With the crowns of gods and demigods,
Bowing down before your lotus feet,
To you who liberate from all misfortune,
Mother Tārā, I offer this homage. ^10-1

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^10-2]]

*[not yet translated]* ^10-2

![[bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག#^10-3]]

This captivating food, endowed with a hundred flavors,
Is perfectly prepared and offered with faith to the Victors and their Heirs;
Through this, may all these wandering beings
Possess great wealth and enjoy the food of meditative concentration. ^10-3
